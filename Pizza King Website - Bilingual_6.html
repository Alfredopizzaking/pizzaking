Our Story

Family-run on Atlantic Avenue since 1962

Pizza King has been slinging pies, subs and wings out of the same red-brick corner for over sixty years. The oval sign, the green-white-red awning, the walk-up window — it all still looks the way regulars remember it, even as the menu grew to fit the neighborhood: Spanish plates and tacos next to cheesesteaks and calzones, breakfast served all morning, and a fryer that doesn't quit till closing.

We deliver, we're open late, and whether it's a slow Tuesday or a packed boardwalk weekend, the order pad looks about the same: pizza, wings, and whatever combo gets you the most food for the money.
